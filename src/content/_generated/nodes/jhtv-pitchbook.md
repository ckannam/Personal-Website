On the capital-strategy team at Johns Hopkins Technology Ventures, I map venture, corporate, and philanthropic funders for early-stage Hopkins spinouts and translate deep research into investment-ready narratives. I built a matching tool that connects each university technology to the investors most likely to fund it — including the Hopkins alumni inside those funds — drawn from PitchBook data. A side project that became part of the team's workflow.
