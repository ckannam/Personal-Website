My Graduation-with-Distinction thesis in Dr. Elizabeth Marsh's memory lab studies metacognition — specifically the Illusion of Explanatory Depth: people believe they understand things far better than they do, until they try to explain them. I run an IRB-approved study on how editing depth reshapes that illusion. (It is also, quietly, the science behind the self-editing knowledge system I run my own life on.)
