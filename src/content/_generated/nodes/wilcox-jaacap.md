As a research assistant in the Wilcox Lab at Johns Hopkins, I contributed to a peer-reviewed systematic review and meta-analysis in the *Journal of the American Academy of Child & Adolescent Psychiatry* — synthesizing 19 psychological-autopsy samples (958 youth who died by suicide, 1,102 controls, across 8 countries) to weigh 26 risk factors. Published research on a question that matters.
