My first-author poster from Duke Anesthesiology's CIPHER / Devinney Lab: in older surgical patients, greater preoperative microstructural damage in the posterior cingulate cortex (measured with NODDI) tracked with reduced intraoperative EEG alpha power under anesthesia — a possible structure-function marker of vulnerability to postoperative delirium.
