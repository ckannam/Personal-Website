title: Who I am
---
I came to Duke pre-med and finished the courses. But the deeper I went into neuroscience, the more I saw brilliant insights that never reached the people they could help. The science was there; the bridge to the world was missing. As AI made raw knowledge cheap to summon, building that bridge became the bigger way to make an impact. So I learned to do both: the science, and the systems that carry it to people.
